  ☐ evidence_refs (list of "fact:FACT_ID" or "chunk:CHUNK_ID")

FOR GROUNDING (pronouns, people, dates):
  ☐ grounding.references list shows all resolved pronouns/names
    - "I" → your_user_id or display name (NOT raw UUID)
    - "you" → recipient or display name
    - "Rohan" → "Rohan" (display name visible, ideally with entity_id in tooltip)
  ☐ grounding.temporal_expressions (list of raw temporal phrases, e.g., ["6pm", "tomorrow"])
  ☐ Temporal_start normalized to ISO format (e.g., "2026-04-13T10:00:00+00:00")

FOR PATHS & HOP TRACKING (multi-hop queries):
  ☐ hop_count visible (should be >1 for complex queries)
  ☐ path_summary or relationships showing the edge chain
  ☐ Each relationship shows: source_node → edge_type → target_node

FOR SUPERSESSION (corrections & updates):
  ☐ If a fact was updated: see PRIOR fact (status: SUPERSEDED) and CURRENT fact
  ☐ Reason for supersession visible (e.g., "Correction posted 2026-04-13")

FOR LONG MODE:
  ☐ answer_payload.mode = "long"
  ☐ reason_code = "explicit_long" (user asked for detail)
  ☐ explanation clearly states why long mode was triggered
  ☐ bullets populated with structured details (not just 1 bullet)

FOR PREVIEW CLAIMS (SAIA disabled/skipped):
  ☐ If SAIA processing disabled: saia_status = "disabled"
  ☐ preview_claims populated (tentative extractions shown for reference)
  ☐ Clearly labeled: "PREVIEW ONLY - Not canonicalized" or similar
